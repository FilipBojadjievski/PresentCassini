## Q&A bullets (after the 4:00, not part of the script)

- **Accuracy?** AUC 0.9524 on time-series holdout. R² 0.737 on hospital surge.
- **Trigger?** RRI > 70 for 5 consecutive days **AND** DO < 5.0 mg/L **AND** pH < 7.95 — all three must align.
- **Pilot?** Ligurian coast — Genoa specifically. 2005 outbreak: 200+ ER admissions in days.
- **Why drop Sentinel-3?** CDSE doesn't expose L2 WFR with CHL_NN. CMEMS chlorophyll-a is a superior source — daily back to 1999, no cloud gaps.
- **Hospital data?** Synthetic, calibrated to Florida Kirkpatrick priors (54% surge). Italian hospital data is a Phase 2 calibration target.
- **IoT?** Mock data for MVP — five sensors LIG_001 to LIG_005. Hardware deployment is Phase 2.
- **B2B2B insurance?** Sustainability layer, **not the headline.** Lead is always: "we detect biological coastal pollution from space."
